The cheap prevention

Risers, lids and filters

The least expensive work I do, and the work that stops most of the expensive work ever being needed.

Why this matters

A tank you can reach is a tank that gets looked after.

Most septic systems in Johnston County are buried a foot or two down with no way to get at them. So nobody opens them, nobody cleans the filter, and the first time anyone digs is when something has already gone wrong.

Risers bring the openings up to ground level with a lid you can lift. After that, a yearly filter clean takes minutes instead of an afternoon with a shovel.

The three jobs

Small work that prevents large work.

Risers

Fitted onto the tank openings and brought up flush with the lawn, with a lid that sits level and doesn’t need digging out. Usually done at the same time as locating a tank nobody could find.

Cracked lids and risers

A crack doesn’t automatically mean a replacement. Plastic welding repairs the existing riser or lid properly, which is cheaper and holds.

The effluent filter

This is the one almost nobody knows about. It sits in the tank outlet and it slowly packs solid. Cleaned once a year it costs you nothing but the time. Left alone, it is one of the most common reasons a house starts backing up and someone gets told the drain field has failed.

If you take one thing from this page: ask whoever services your system when the filter was last cleaned. If nobody can answer, that is the first thing to check.
