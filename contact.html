Common questions

Before you call

Do you pump tanks?

Repairs and system work are what I do. For a straight pump-out I'll put you on to someone who does that — often we end up on the same job.

How soon can you get here?

Most jobs get seen the same day they're called in. If it's an emergency, say so when you call.

Will you tell me what it costs first?

Yes. You get the price before the work starts, and the quote holds even if the job turns out bigger than expected.

My alarm is going off. Is that bad?

Usually it means a pump or a float has stopped, not that the system has failed. Stop running water where you can, and call.

Nobody knows where our tank is. Can you still help?

Yes, that's a common one. I'll locate it, dig the lid out and show you, and risers can go on afterwards so it doesn't happen again.

How often does the filter need cleaning?

About once a year. It's the cheapest part of the system and the one most likely to cause a backup when it's neglected.
